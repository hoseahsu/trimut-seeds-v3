A coach ranks trainees by recorded scores.

Marek is a fun loving lad. His fellow trainees are very competitive and are striving for getting a good rank, while he loves seeing them fight for it.  The results are going to be out soon. He is anxious to know what is the rank of each of his fellow trainees.   

Rank of i'th trainee = 1+ (Number of trainees having strictly greater scores than him. Trainees with equal scores are ranked same.)    

Can you help Marek figure out rank of all his fellow trainees?  

Input:
The first line contains an integer N, denoting the number of trainees in the training group.
The second line contains N space separated integers, Mi are the scores of the i'th trainee.  

Ouput:
Print N space separated integers i.e., ranks of the trainees in a new line.  

Constraints:
1 ≤ N ≤ 1000
1 ≤ Mi ≤ 1000

SAMPLE INPUT
4
62 96 82 55

SAMPLE OUTPUT
3 1 2 4
