An observer follows an athlete across successive events.

Nolan adores competitive athletics. He can't write event software but he loves to watch the meets' progress. Nolan even has a favorite athlete and Nolan pays special attention to him.

One day Nolan decided to collect the results of all meets where his favorite athlete participated and track the progress of his coolness. For each meet where this athlete participated, he wrote out a single non-negative number — the number of points his favorite athlete earned in the meet. Nolan wrote out the points for the meet in the order, in which the meets run (naturally, no two meets ran simultaneously).

Nolan considers a athlete's performance in a meet amazing in two situations: he can break either his best or his worst performance record. First, it is amazing if during the meet the athlete earns strictly more points that he earned on each past meet. Second, it is amazing if during the meet the athlete earns strictly less points that he earned on each past meet. A athlete's first meet isn't considered amazing. Now he wants to count the number of amazing performances the athlete had throughout his whole history of participating in meets. But the list of earned points turned out long and Nolan can't write software... That's why he asks you to help him.

Input

The first line contains the single integer n (1 ≤ n ≤ 1000) — the number of meets where the athlete participated.

The next line contains n space-separated non-negative integer numbers — they are the points which the athlete has earned. The points are given in the chronological order. All points do not exceed 10000.

Output

Print the single number — the number of amazing performances the athlete has had during his whole history of participating in the meets.

Examples

Input

5
100 50 200 150 200


Output

2


Input

10
4664 6496 5814 7010 5762 5736 6944 4850 3698 7242


Output

4

Note

In the first sample the performances number 2 and 3 are amazing.

In the second sample the performances number 2, 4, 9 and 10 are amazing.
