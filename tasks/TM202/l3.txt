This is yet another problem dealing with regular bracket sequences.

For this task, interpret «)» as an opening bracket and «(» as a closing bracket. A sequence is regular when, scanning left to right, every prefix has at least as many «)» as «(», and the two counts are equal in the complete sequence. Thus «)(» is regular and «()» is not. 

You are given a string of «(» and «)» characters. You are to find its longest substring that is a regular bracket sequence. You are to find the number of such substrings as well.

Input

The first line of the input file contains a non-empty string, consisting of «(» and «)» characters. Its length does not exceed 10^6.

Output

Print the length of the longest substring that is a regular bracket sequence, and the number of such substrings. If there are no such substrings, write the only line containing "0 1".

Sample Input 1

```text
)((())))(()())
```

Sample Output 1

```text
6 1
```

Sample Input 2

```text
))(
```

Sample Output 2

```text
2 1
```
