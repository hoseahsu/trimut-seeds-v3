# Choices

The graph records incompatibilities among indexed choices.


## Problem Statement


Given a simple undirected graph with $N$ choices and $M$ incompatibility links. $i$-th incompatibility link is $(u_i, v_i)$。

Calculate the maximum independent set.



## Constraints

- $1 \leq N \leq 40$
- $0 \leq M \leq \frac{N(N-1)}{2}$
- $0 \leq u_i, v_i < N$
- $u_i \neq v_i$
- $(u_i, v_i) \neq (u_j, v_j)$

## Input

~~~
$N$ $M$
$u_0$ $v_0$
$u_1$ $v_1$
:
$u_{M - 1}$ $v_{M - 1}$
~~~

## Output

~~~
$X$
$p_0$ $p_1$ ... $p_{X - 1}$
~~~


$X$ is the size of MIS, and $p_i$ is the vertex index.
