A contest ranks crews by completed exercises and penalty time.

Another skills trial is over. You got hold of the contest's final results table. The table has the following data. For each crew we are shown two numbers: the number of exercises and the total penalty time. However, for no crew we are shown its final place.

You know the rules of comparing the results of two given crews very well. Let's say that crew a solved pa exercises with total penalty time ta and crew b solved pb exercises with total penalty time tb. Crew a gets a higher place than crew b in the end, if it either solved more exercises on the contest, or solved the same number of exercises but in less total time. In other words, crew a gets a higher place than crew b in the final results' table if either pa > pb, or pa = pb and ta < tb. 

It is considered that the crews that solve the same number of exercises with the same penalty time share all corresponding places. More formally, let's say there is a group of x crews that solved the same number of exercises with the same penalty time. Let's also say that y crews performed better than the crews from this group. In this case all crews from the group share places y + 1, y + 2, ..., y + x. The crews that performed worse than the crews from this group, get their places in the results table starting from the y + x + 1-th place.

Your task is to count what number of crews from the given list shared the k-th place. 

Input

The first line contains two integers n and k (1 ≤ k ≤ n ≤ 50). Then n lines contain the description of the crews: the i-th line contains two integers pi and ti (1 ≤ pi, ti ≤ 50) — the number of solved exercises and the total penalty time of the i-th crew, correspondingly. All numbers in the lines are separated by spaces. 

Output

In the only line print the sought number of crews that got the k-th place in the final results' table.

Examples

Input

7 2
4 10
4 10
4 10
3 20
2 1
2 1
1 10


Output

3


Input

5 4
3 1
3 1
5 3
3 1
3 1


Output

4

Note

The final results' table for the first sample is: 

  * 1-3 places — 4 solved exercises, the penalty time equals 10 
  * 4 place — 3 solved exercises, the penalty time equals 20 
  * 5-6 places — 2 solved exercises, the penalty time equals 1 
  * 7 place — 1 solved exercise, the penalty time equals 10 



The table shows that the second place is shared by the crews that solved 4 exercises with penalty time 10. There are 3 such crews.

The final table for the second sample is:

  * 1 place — 5 solved exercises, the penalty time equals 3 
  * 2-5 places — 3 solved exercises, the penalty time equals 1 



The table shows that the fourth place is shared by the crews that solved 3 exercises with penalty time 1. There are 4 such crews.
