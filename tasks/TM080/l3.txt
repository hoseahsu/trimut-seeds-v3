Nadeko's birthday is approaching! As she decorated the room for the party, a long garland of Dianthus-shaped paper pieces was placed on a prominent part of the wall. Brother Koyomi will like it!

Still unsatisfied with the garland, Nadeko decided to polish it again. The garland has n pieces numbered from 1 to n from left to right, and the i-th piece has a colour si, denoted by a lowercase English letter. For a plan (m,c), Nadeko must select a contiguous subsegment containing exactly m pieces whose initial colour differs from c. She repaints precisely those m pieces to c; all other pieces are left unchanged. The score of the plan is the length of the longest eligible selected subsegment, or 0 if none exists.

For instance, let's say the garland is represented by "kooomo", and Brother Koyomi's favourite colour is "o". Among all subsegments containing pieces of "o" only, "ooo" is the longest, with a length of 3. For the changed task, if m=1 and c="o", the subsegment "ooomo" is eligible and has length 5; no length-6 subsegment has exactly one mismatch.

But problem arises as Nadeko is unsure about Brother Koyomi's favourite colour, and has swaying ideas on the amount of work to do. She has q plans on this, each of which can be expressed as a pair of an integer mi and a lowercase letter ci, meanings of which are explained above. You are to find out the maximum eligible selected-subsegment length for each plan.

Input

The first line of input contains a positive integer n (1 ≤ n ≤ 1 500) — the length of the garland.

The second line contains n lowercase English letters s1s2... sn as a string — the initial colours of paper pieces on the garland.

The third line contains a positive integer q (1 ≤ q ≤ 200 000) — the number of plans Nadeko has.

The next q lines describe one plan each: the i-th among them contains an integer mi (1 ≤ mi ≤ n) — the exact number of mismatching pieces in the selected subsegment, followed by a space, then by a lowercase English letter ci — Koyomi's possible favourite colour.

Output

Output q lines: for each work plan, output one line containing an integer — the largest eligible selected-subsegment length, or 0 if no such subsegment exists.

```text
4 m

```
