A group distributes riders among single and paired transport pods.

Marek is really keen on travelling. Recently he heard about pod riding activity near his town and became very excited about it, so he joined a party of pod riders.

Now the party is ready to start its journey, but firstly they have to choose transport pods. There are 2·n people in the group (including Marek), and they have exactly n - 1 tandem transport pods (each of which, obviously, can carry two people) and 2 single transport pods. i-th person's weight is wi, and weight is an important matter in pod riding — if the difference between the weights of two people that sit in the same tandem transport pod is too large, then it can crash. And, of course, people want to distribute their seats in transport pods in order to minimize the chances that transport pods will crash.

Formally, the instability of a single transport pod is always 0, and the instability of a tandem transport pod is the absolute difference between weights of the people that are in this transport pod. Instability of the whole journey is the total instability of all transport pods.

Help the party to determine minimum possible total instability! 

Input

The first line contains one number n (2 ≤ n ≤ 50).

The second line contains 2·n integer numbers w1, w2, ..., w2n, where wi is weight of person i (1 ≤ wi ≤ 1000).

Output

Print minimum possible total instability.

Examples

Input

2
1 2 3 4


Output

1


Input

4
1 3 4 6 3 4 100 200


Output

5
