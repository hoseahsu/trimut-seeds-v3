Two collectors alternately take valued tiles.

We have N valued tiles. A number a_i is written on the i-th valued tile.
Mira and Nolan will play a game using these valued tiles. In this game, Mira and Nolan alternately take one valued tile. Mira goes first.
The game ends when all the valued tiles are taken by the two players, and the score of each player is the sum of the numbers written on the valued tiles he/she has taken. When both players take the optimal strategy to maximize their scores, find Mira's score minus Nolan's score.

Constraints

* N is an integer between 1 and 100 (inclusive).
* a_i \ (1 \leq i \leq N) is an integer between 1 and 100 (inclusive).

Input

Input is given from Standard Input in the following format:


N
a_1 a_2 a_3 ... a_N


Output

Print Mira's score minus Nolan's score when both players take the optimal strategy to maximize their scores.

Examples

Input

2
3 1


Output

2


Input

3
2 7 4


Output

5


Input

4
20 18 2 18


Output

18
