A vendor sells full and combined snack platters.

"Pizza At", a fast food chain, offers three kinds of platter: "A-pizza", "B-pizza" and "AB-pizza". A-platter and B-platter are completely different platters, and AB-platter is one half of A-platter and one half of B-platter combined together. The prices of one A-platter, B-platter and AB-platter are A yen, B yen and C yen (yen is the currency of Japan), respectively.

Nolan needs to prepare X A-platters and Y B-platters for a party tonight. He can only obtain these platters by directly buying A-platters and B-platters, or buying two AB-platters and then rearrange them into one A-platter and one B-platter. At least how much money does he need for this? It is fine to have more platters than necessary by rearranging platters.

Constraints

* 1 ≤ A, B, C ≤ 5000
* 1 ≤ X, Y ≤ 10^5
* All values in input are integers.

Input

Input is given from Standard Input in the following format:


A B C X Y


Output

Print the minimum amount of money required to prepare X A-platters and Y B-platters.

Examples

Input

1500 2000 1600 3 2


Output

7900


Input

1500 2000 1900 3 2


Output

8500


Input

1500 2000 500 90000 100000


Output

100000000
