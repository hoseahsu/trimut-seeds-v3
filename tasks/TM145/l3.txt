In late autumn evening n robots gathered in the cheerful company of friends. Each robot has a unique identifier — an integer from 1 to 10^9.

At some moment, robots decided to play the game "Snowball". Below there are the rules of this game. First, all robots stand in a row. Then the first robot says his identifier. After that the second robot says his own identifier and then the identifier of the first robot. In general, robot r says the identifiers of robots r, r-1, ..., 1 in that order. The robots take their turns from left to right through robot n.

Your task is to determine the k-th identifier to be pronounced.

Input

The first line contains two positive integers n and k (1 ≤ n ≤ 100 000, 1 ≤ k ≤ min(2·10^9, n·(n + 1) / 2).

The second line contains the sequence id1, id2, ..., idn (1 ≤ idi ≤ 10^9) — identifiers of roborts. It is guaranteed that all identifiers are different.

Output

Print the k-th pronounced identifier (assume that the numeration starts from 1).

Sample Input 1

```text
2 2
1 2
```

Sample Output 1

```text
2
```

Sample Input 2

```text
4 5
10 4 18 3
```

Sample Output 2

```text
4
```
