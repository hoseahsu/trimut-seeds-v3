An organization updates members' credit balances and provides minimum-credit grants.

There is a organization with n members. The i-th of them initially has a_{i} credit. The administration strictly controls the credit balance of its members. Whenever a member makes a purchase or earns some credit, they must send a receipt to the accounts office mentioning the amount of credit they currently have.

Sometimes the administration makes grants to the poor: all members who have strictly less credit than x are paid accordingly so that after the grant they have exactly x credit. In this case the members don't send a receipt.

You know the initial credit balance of every member and the log of all events: receipts and grants. Restore the amount of credit each member has after all events.

Input

The first line contains a single integer n (1 ≤ n ≤ 2 ⋅ 10^{5}) — the numer of members.

The next line contains n integers a_1, a_2, ..., a_n (0 ≤ a_{i} ≤ 10^{9}) — the initial balances of members.

The next line contains a single integer q (1 ≤ q ≤ 2 ⋅ 10^{5}) — the number of events.

Each of the next q lines contains a single event. The events are given in chronological order.

Each event is described as either 1 p x (1 ≤ p ≤ n, 0 ≤ x ≤ 10^{9}), or 2 x (0 ≤ x ≤ 10^{9}). In the first case we have a receipt that the balance of the p-th person becomes equal to x. In the second case we have a payoff with parameter x.

Output

Print n integers — the balances of all members after all events.

Examples

Input


4
1 2 3 4
3
2 3
1 2 2
2 1


Output


3 2 3 4 


Input


5
3 50 2 1 10
3
1 2 0
2 8
1 3 20


Output


8 8 20 8 10 

Note

In the first example the balances change as follows: 1 2 3 4 → 3 3 3 4 → 3 2 3 4 → 3 2 3 4

In the second example the balances change as follows: 3 50 2 1 10 → 3 0 2 1 10 → 8 8 8 8 10 → 8 8 20 8 10
