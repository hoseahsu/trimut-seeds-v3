An observer selects coordinate markers along a line.

Little Marek likes coordinate markers a lot. Recently his mentor has presented him n coordinate markers lying on the line OX. Now Marek is wondering in how many ways he can choose three distinct coordinate markers so that the distance between the two farthest of them doesn't exceed d.

Note that the order of the coordinate markers inside the group of three chosen coordinate markers doesn't matter.

Input

The first line contains two integers: n and d (1 ≤ n ≤ 10^5; 1 ≤ d ≤ 10^9). The next line contains n integers x1, x2, ..., xn, their absolute value doesn't exceed 10^9 — the x-coordinates of the coordinate markers that Marek has got.

It is guaranteed that the coordinates of the coordinate markers in the input strictly increase.

Output

Print a single integer — the number of groups of three coordinate markers, where the distance between two farthest coordinate markers doesn't exceed d.

Please do not use the %lld specifier to read or write 64-bit integers in С++. It is preferred to use the cin, cout streams or the %I64d specifier.

Examples

Input

4 3
1 2 3 4


Output

4


Input

4 2
-3 -2 -1 0


Output

2


Input

5 19
1 10 20 30 50


Output

1

Note

In the first sample any group of three coordinate markers meets our conditions.

In the seconds sample only 2 groups of three coordinate markers meet our conditions: {-3, -2, -1} and {-2, -1, 0}.

In the third sample only one group does: {1, 10, 20}.
